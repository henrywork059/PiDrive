Camera input

Stop PiSD before choosing a source. Replay is open-loop: vehicle motion cannot change recorded images.

Camera capture fault

Inject a capture failure while PiSD is running. Removing the fault does not rearm AI.

Virtual motor disconnect

Reject nonzero virtual output and clear delayed motor commands. Stop PiSD to recover. PiSD commanded values may differ from applied virtual output.
